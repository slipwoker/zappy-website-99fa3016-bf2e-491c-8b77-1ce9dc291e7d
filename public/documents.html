Questions about sharing documents

Find answers to common questions about how Shabos makes document sharing simple and secure.

How do I control who can access my shared documents?

Shabos gives you complete control over document access. You can set permissions for each file individually, choosing between view-only, comment, or full edit access. Share via secure links with optional password protection, or invite specific users by email. Access can be revoked at any time from your sharing dashboard.

Can I customize how my documents are presented to viewers?

Yes, Shabos offers multiple presentation options for your shared documents. Choose between grid or list view, add cover images, include descriptions, and organize files into collections. You can also customize the viewer interface with your branding colors and logo for a professional appearance.

How does Shabos help me organize large document libraries?

Our platform includes powerful organization tools including folders, tags, and smart collections. Use the search function to find documents instantly by name, content, or metadata. Create custom categories and apply batch operations to manage multiple files efficiently. All documents are automatically indexed for quick retrieval.

What's the workflow for sharing documents with external collaborators?

Sharing is streamlined and secure. Upload your document, set permissions, and generate a shareable link or send direct invitations. External collaborators receive instant access without needing to create an account. Track who views or downloads your files, receive notifications of activity, and manage all shared documents from one central dashboard.

What support is available if I encounter issues with document sharing?

Shabos provides comprehensive support through multiple channels. Access our detailed knowledge base with step-by-step guides, video tutorials, and troubleshooting tips. Contact our support team via email or live chat during business hours. Premium users receive priority support with dedicated account managers and faster response times.

Need a clean document hub?

We'd love to discuss your document library and sharing goals. Whether you're managing team files, client resources, or educational materials, Shabos can help you create a seamless sharing experience.
